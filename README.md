# BRM5 HQ Planner

A community-made headquarters planner for **Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5**. Track research, cash, resources, staff and deliveries, and keep a plan for each HQ visit.

Enter your current HQ details and choose a goal. The planner uses research prerequisites, costs, timers and available power to compare research schedules, plan short follow-ups within each visit and suggest Operations assignments and deliveries. Record what you do in-game to keep future sessions up to date.

Progress saves in your browser, with optional Google or Discord sign-in to sync between devices. You can also export and import backups. This is a manual tracker; it does not connect to Roblox or perform game actions.

[Open the planner](https://brm5-hq-planner.vercel.app/)
