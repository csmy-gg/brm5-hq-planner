BRM5 HQ PLANNER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

BRM5 HQ Planner is an independent community tool for planning research and tracking a player's in-game headquarters. This policy explains how the planner handles information when you use it as a guest, sign in, or contact support.

Who operates this service

The BRM5 HQ Planner operator maintains this website and its account-save database. Contact the operator at brm5hqplanner@googlegroups.com for support, privacy questions or account-deletion requests.

The planner is not an official Roblox or Platinum Five service and does not connect to your Roblox account or play the game for you.

Information the planner handles

  • HQ information you enter: in-game cash and resources, staff and assignments, completed and active research, deliveries, timers, visit schedules, goals, settings, and action history.
  • Sign-in information: if you choose Google or Discord sign-in, Supabase Authentication processes your provider identifier, email address and basic profile information made available by that provider, which may include your display name and profile image. It also manages the session information needed to keep you signed in.
  • Browser storage: the planner stores your guest HQ, account-specific device copies, pending saves, recovery copies, appearance and guide preferences, and sign-in session information in your browser.
  • Technical requests: the hosting and database providers process network information such as IP addresses, request details and browser information to deliver and operate the service.
  • Website analytics: Vercel Web Analytics collects aggregate planner page-view and visitor statistics, including referral sources, approximate location, browser, operating system and device type. It uses a request-derived visitor hash that is discarded after 24 hours, without analytics cookies. Our integration removes query strings and URL fragments and excludes sign-in callback pages, preview deployments and local development. It does not send HQ profiles, balances, staff, research selections, email addresses or account identifiers as analytics properties.
  • Support correspondence: if you email support, the operator receives the email address and message information you send.

Google sign-in uses basic identity scopes: openid, email and profile. The planner does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files or Google Contacts. Your Google or Discord password is entered with that provider; it is not entered into or stored by the planner.

How information is used

Information is used to calculate your session plan, remember your progress and preferences, authenticate your account, synchronize your HQ between devices, recover conflicting saves, operate the service and respond to support requests.

Information received through Google sign-in is used for authentication and the account-save features. The planner does not use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models. Aggregate website analytics help the operator understand traffic and device usage; the planner does not add advertising trackers or record screen activity.

Services involved

Vercel hosts the website and processes website analytics. Supabase provides authentication and the database that holds account saves. Google or Discord handles the sign-in you select. The public support address uses Google Groups. Those services process information needed to provide their respective functions. Read Vercel's Web Analytics privacy explanation for its analytics data practices.

Account HQ profiles are not publicly listed. Database access rules restrict player access to their own account's profile. The operator can administer the service and its database, including when responding to support or deletion requests.

Provider policies: Vercel privacy policy and Supabase privacy policy. Infrastructure and service providers may process information in different countries; this website does not promise that all processing remains in one country.

Storage, retention and your choices

You can use the planner without an account. Guest progress stays in that browser unless you export it or choose to save it to an account. Account profiles remain in the account-save database until replaced or deleted by the operator, including in response to a verified deletion request.

Signing out ends this browser's sign-in session and returns the interface to its separate guest HQ. It does not delete your cloud account, and account-specific device drafts and recovery copies may remain in browser storage. Clearing site data removes browser copies and preferences, but does not delete cloud data. Export any progress you want to retain before clearing site data.

You can export your HQ using Export backup. To request access, correction or deletion of cloud account information, email brm5hqplanner@googlegroups.com from the address associated with the account. The operator may request proof of account ownership. Deleting cloud data does not remove backups you downloaded or copies on other devices. Service-provider logs and backups follow the providers' retention practices.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the planner's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
