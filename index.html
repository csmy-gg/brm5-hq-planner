NEW HERE?Get your tracker readyFive setup steps, then one check-in each visit. Open this guide any time.⌄

Open BRM5’s HQ alongside this page and copy what the game shows. The plan recalculates when you save. This tracker records your actions; it does not connect to Roblox or play for you.

Start with your HQ. Everyone starts with zero balances, no staff and no completed research. Import a backup or follow these five steps.

  1. Enter your cash and resources

    In Resources & money, enter your actual cash and all four resource amounts, then choose Save current stock. CM = Construction Materials, PM = Precious Metals, F = Fuel, E = Equipment.

  2. Match your staff and Operations assignments

    In Staff & readiness, enter staff by department and rank. Include injured staff in the total and mark them unavailable. Copy your HQ’s raid and win chances, then Save staffing. Enter who is currently producing each resource and Save assignments.

    After a raid, update injuries or deaths. Return recovered staff in-game, then reduce the unavailable count here.

  3. Record what is already researched

    Select everything you have already researched below. Check the items completed in your game, then save.

    Select my completed research

    Checked means completed. Unchecking a completed item sets it to Not started. Unchecked active items stay in progress. This never changes cash or resources. Select completed items from every branch, including Products. Active items keep their existing timers until you change them.

    On the Research board, find each item and open Adjust recorded status. Set it to Not started, In progress or Completed. These status corrections leave your balances unchanged. For active research, use Edit remaining time to match the game.

    For new starts, use Start research. Check Costs already reflected in my balances if you entered balances after paying for that item.

  4. Check deliveries already on the way

    Under Send a delivery, review recorded deliveries and adjust their remaining time. Record only deliveries you actually send; a dispatch removes resources, and collection adds cash. To record an existing delivery whose resources have already left your saved stock, check Resources already left my recorded stock in Send a delivery.

  5. Choose your schedule and goal

    In Planning settings, enter your HQ’s total Research power, cash reserve, first visit time and hours between visits, then Update settings. Check your storage setting and choose a production uptime estimate to allow for interruptions. A reserve protects that amount from planned research spending. Visit times use your selected timezone. The suggested 12-hour interval and 90% production uptime are editable planning assumptions.

    Use Working toward in Current session to choose all research, one item, cash or a resource goal. Check Session plan for the next recommended actions.

At each visit

Do the actions in BRM5, then choose Mark session as completed to open your check-in. Record recoveries, collections, research starts and assignments there; saved actions update the other sections automatically.

If you enter your final in-game balances first, use the relevant already included options so costs or payouts are counted once. Update cash and resources after purchases, production or raids. Choose Save updates & finish session only when you are done. The current visit stays in place until you finish it.

Keep your progress: guest data is saved in this browser. Sign in to sync your account between devices. Use Export backup regularly and Import backup on another browser or device. Action history and Undo last change can recover a mistaken entry.

    Planning settings

    Changing timezone alone preserves all deadlines. On a repeated autumn hour, the first occurrence is used. Editing the visit date reschedules the current unconfirmed session. The interval sets the spacing of later visits.

    Staff & readiness

    Staff counts by rank. Adjust these after hiring or a death.

    “Unavailable” includes injured staff being treated. They reduce production or capacity until you return them. Training counts are tracked for your records. Research power is entered from the game because it depends on ranks across three departments. Doctors and Defense use your observed game readings; healing times and deaths are not predictable from the wiki.

    Current Operations assignments

    Used to project production until your next visit. Mixed ranks use the department’s average rate. Uptime includes the disruption allowance you choose.

    Research board

    Browse the full research tree, including products and ammunition.

    Start research

    Choose the item you just started in-game. Its costs, timer and shared power update together.

    Leave unchecked to deduct the research cost once. Check it if your saved cash and resources already include that expense.

    Active research

    Research power is shared across active items. Complete earlier prerequisites before starting later research.
    Research guide

    The four resource columns are Construction Materials, Precious Metals, Fuel and Equipment. Each active item uses part of your total research power until you record its completion.

    Category nodes such as Manufacturing, Education and Programs are navigation only. Product and ammunition prerequisites appear on their research cards. Confirm completed research before starting an item that depends on it.

    Update staff, balances, injuries and raid results after each visit to keep your plan accurate.

    Your session plan

    Your current session stays here until confirmed. Later visits are forecasts based on your latest snapshot.

    Every visit: treat injured staff, return recovered staff, collect ready deliveries, confirm stock, then research and dispatch. Visits follow elapsed hours, including daylight-saving clock changes.

    Action history

    Last 50 saved changes, retained when you refresh.

    Restoring an earlier point also reverses changes made after it. Each restore creates a new history entry, so you can undo the restoration too.
